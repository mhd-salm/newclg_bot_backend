Introduction 
The establishment of The New College in 1951 marks an 
important milestone in the educational empowerment and 
renaissance of the Muslim minority community in Tamil Nadu. 
The college was founded to address the deficiency of higher 
education opportunities for the Muslims in Southern India. The 
Muslim Educational Association of Southern India (MEASI), 
the parent body of the college, commenced its educational 
endeavours in 1902 inspired by influential reformers within the 
community such as Sir Syed Ahmed Khan. The college started 
its operations in a colonial-era structure located within the 
‘Association Gardens’ on 2 July 1951 after securing affiliation 
fromthe University of Madras. It was named after one of the 
constituentcolleges of the University of Oxford in the United 
Kingdom,founded in 1379. 
In its early years, the College exclusively provided Pre- 
University Courses. However, in 1956, it expanded its 
offerings to include Undergraduate programmes. These 
included a B.A. programme in History, Politics, and 
Economics, as well as a B.Sc. programme in Mathematics, 
Chemistry, and Zoology. The College then introduced its first 
postgraduate programme, M.A. in Arabic, in 1965. Presently, 
the College offers a wide range of programmes, including 29 
Undergraduate, 15 Postgraduate, and 11 Ph.D. programmes. 
These programmes are conducted in two shifts and cater to 
approximately 7000 students. While the College was initially 
established to empower the Muslim community through 
education, it has since welcomed students from all 
communities, both nationally and internationally.

The college achieved Autonomous status in 2007. It has 
undergone four rounds of accreditation by the NAAC 
(National Assessment and Accreditation Council) and has 
received an A++ Grade (CGPA 3.61 out of 4) in the most 
recent (4th) cycle. The college actively participates in the NIRF 
ranking and is currently positioned in the 151 to 200 rank band. 
The Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) of the college 
plays a crucial role in preparing the institution for various 
assessments, rankings and accreditations. Being autonomous, 
the college regularly updates its curriculum to align with the 
local, regional, national, and international requirements, as 
well as the evolving employment opportunities for graduates 
worldwide. 
The college actively involves its students in various 
curricular and co-curricular activities with the objective of 
making them well-rounded individuals and responsible 
citizens. The Social Service wings of the college like the NSS, 
NCC, Rotaract Club, Citizen Consumer Club, NEST, Red 
Ribbon Club, Youth Red Cross and Departmental Outreach 
clubs have been engaging in various social and developmental 
initiatives in the neighbourhood areas of the college and 
adopted villages. The college adopted five villages under the 
Unnat Bharat Abhiyan scheme. All the social service activities 
of the college are coordinated by the New College Social 
Service (NCSS) centre. 
The Career Guidance and Placement Cell regularly 
organises employability skills training programmes and 
arranges campus recruitment drives for the outgoing students. 
A course on Employability Skills is offered to all the first year 
UG students with the objective of orienting them towards their 
career plans and to prepare them for campus placement in their 
final year. The Competitive Examination Cell of the college 
also provides training to aspiring students for various 
competitive examinations for job and higher studies in 
collaboration with different departments.

The facilities available in the campus also include a hostel 
with capacity of over 550 students. Besides providing modern 
amenities like spacious rooms, clean and hygienic bathrooms 
for the boarders, the new college hostel premises are beautified 
with lush green lawns and gardens. The other green initiatives 
in the hostel campus include a biogas plant, a grey water 
recycling plant, an organic vegetable garden, and a RO plant. 
The New College library is one of the largest college 
libraries in the state with over 1.4 lakh books in different 
categories.The library is digitised with an Integrated Library 
Management System. In addition to general and subject related 
books, reference section, books and materials for competitive 
examinations, magazines, and scholarly journals, the library 
provides internet browsing facility and access to digital 
resources. The Book Bank facility available in the library allows 
financially backward students to borrow and keep up to six books 
for the entire semester. 
The sports amenities available in the campus include a 
spacious playground with facilities for almost all popular 
games, and a Gymnasium for fitness training and practice. 
Sports meets are conducted by the college every year to identify 
and nurture talented students in sports and games. Besides, 
students of the college are encouraged to participate in various 
sports events conducted at University, State and National 
levels. A few of our students have also been attending 
international level competitions. Recently, the college has 
modernised its basketball court, badminton court and cricket 
nets. The college admits students under sports quota every year 
and provides them free education and free boarding and 
lodging. 
In addition to preserving the green cover in the campus, the 
college has set up elaborate provisions for rainwater 
harvesting, solar electricity generation, and an effluent 
treatment plant. The flora in the campus is provided with labels 
containing scientific and vernacular names and QR codes for 
further details.

College Crest 
The Crescent and the Star in the College Crest represent progress 
and light and also embody Islamic Culture. The Lotus represents 
Indian Culture. 
The Torch represents enlightenment and knowledge and the Palm 
represents sweetness and fruitfulness. The Arabic verse is a prayer 
which means “MY LORD, INCREASE ME IN KNOWLEDGE”. 
The background colours of the college crest are green and red. The 
green colour indicates Prosperity and the red colour Sacrifice.

Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) 
The Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) oversees the 
overall quality initiatives of the college. The Cell is responsible for 
formulating the annual strategic plans, preparing the college for 
NAAC Accreditation, submission of data for the annual National 
Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF) and All India Survey of 
Higher Education (AISHE), and extension of Autonomous Status. 
Besides, the Cell responsible for Collecting and analysing feedback 
from various stakeholders, conducting various audits, and promoting 
online learning through MOOC Courses. 
The Director and Deputy Director of IQAC two coordinators 
lead the activities of the cell. The committees that work for IQAC 
include a Steering Committee of the IQAC as per UGC regulations, 
a Data Coordination Team, and a team of Departmental IQAC Aides, 
who serve as Micro Cells of IQAC in their departments.

College Rules and Regulations 
Attendance  
1. Students are expected to attend all the five periods of each 
working day. 
2. A minimum of 75% of attendance is required for all the 
students to become eligible to write the examinations. In 
the case of I year students the minimum required 
percentage of attendance is 80%. 
3. Attendance is marked at the beginning of every hour by the 
respective teachers using the Mobile Application for the 
ERP system used by the college. 
4. All the students have to download the EMBASE Pro Suit 
Mobile App to view their attendance status and other 
details. 
5. Late-comers will not be permitted to enter the class. They 
should go to the general library and engage themselves till 
the session is over and they can attend classes from the next 
hour. 
6. Students are not permitted to leave the college premises 
during working hours. During emergency needs, they are 
required to get permission from the Principal or the Head 
of their Department to leave the campus. 
7. Percentage of attendance is calculated taking into account 
all the classes including Languages, English, Core and Part 
IV courses. 
8. Students who do not attend classes for more than ten days 
without prior permission from Head of the Department 
through their Programme Coordinator will be removed 
from the rolls. A re-admission fee will be levied for the 
reinstatement of their names in the rolls. 
9. Those who secure attendance between 65% and 75% will 
have to pay a condonation fee to become eligible to write 
the Semester examinations. Those who secure attendance 
96 
Handbook & Academic Calendar 2025-2026 
between 50% and 65% will be detained from writing 
examinations. Students with less than 50% attendance will 
be required to repeat the Semester for which they do not 
have adequate attendance as per the University norms. 
10. Students detained in a semester have to pay condonation fee 
to become eligible to sit for the missed-out examinations. 
11. Students who want to avail leave should submit their leave 
applications, preferably in advance, to the respective 
Programme Coordinators. 
12. Leave availed on medical grounds and for various 
extracurricular activities will be accepted only if the 
students produce relevant certificates. Medical certificates 
issued by registered medical practitioners alone will be 
acceptable. 
13. Medical leave will be allowed for a maximum of 14 days in 
a semester. Any leave on medical grounds exceeding 7 days 
requires evidence of hospitalization and medical fitness 
certificate. 
14. Letters of leave for Co-curricular and Extra-curricular 
activities and on-other duty (OD) leave should be endorsed 
by the faculty in charge / Head concerned. 
15. A student is permitted to avail maximum 15 days in a 
semester on other duty (OD) attendance. However, special 
permission may be sought from the Principal for special 
needs like NCC camp for leave beyond 15 days. On the 
completion of such leave, students should submit the 
attendance certificate/evidence.

Code of Conduct  
1. Ragging of any kind is strictly forbidden inside or outside 
the campus. Students caught for ragging will be dealt as per 
the regulations of UGC and State Government. 
2. All students have to submit an online anti-ragging 
undertaking by visiting the following web link: 
https://antiragging.in/affidavit_affiliated_form.php 
3. Identity Card is mandatory to enter the campus. All 
students must wear their Identity Cards during the college 
hours. They are required to show their ID cards to the 
security personnel near the college gate and the college staff 
(both teaching & non-teaching) whenever demanded. Loss 
of Identity Cards should be reported immediately. 
4. Students are expected to be courteous and polite. They 
should not use any abusive language or discriminatory 
expressions to their fellow students, faculty members and 
staff. 
5. Students are not permitted to use Mobile phones inside the 
classrooms, library, seminar halls and examinations halls. 
Phones of students who violate this rule will be confiscated. 
6. Students are forbidden from organizing or attending any 
meeting inside the college campus or collecting money for 
any purpose without prior permission from the Principal. 
7. Students are not permitted to enrol themselves as members 
of any political parties nor are they allowed to take 
part/organize in any political agitations inside or outside the 
campus. 
8. The college campus has been declared as a "No Smoking 
Zone”. Strict disciplinary action will be taken against 
students found indulging in smoking.

Handbook & Academic Calendar 2025-2026 
9. Possession / use of drugs, alcohol and other intoxicants is 
strictly banned in the college campus. 
10. Students who create any form of public nuisance or engage 
in any unlawful activities inside or outside the campus, will 
be dealt with in accordance with the rule of law in the state, 
and such students will be suspended / dismissed from the 
college. The college will not take responsibility for any 
criminal or non-criminal acts committed by students of the 
college outside the college campus. 
11. Students who defame the college in any form using online 
or offline platforms will be dismissed from the college. 
12. The college will not tolerate any breach of academic 
integrity including plagiarism in assignments and copying 
in examinations, and other unethical practices. 
13. Students committing Cyber Crimes (illegal activities on the 
internet) will face disciplinary action including suspension 
/ dismissal from the college. 
14. Possessing / watching pornographic materials / videos / 
photos on mobile phones, Tablet, Computers and other 
devices is strictly banned inside the campus. 
15. Eating, chewing and drinking beverages other than water 
are strictly forbidden inside the classrooms during class 
hours.

Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA) 
Continuous internal assessment carries 25% of marks in 
every course (25/100). There is no minimum pass mark in CIA. 
However, student who scored less than 10 marks in CIA and more 
than 30 marks in External examination (End-Semester) for the same 
paper, are allowed to enhance their CIA marks (for the current papers 
only) in the next semester.

Examination Fees 
In addition to the College fees, all students have to pay the 
required amount of fees for each semester examination. The due 
dates for examination fees are mentioned in the college calendar and 
reminders for the same are sent as circulars by the Principal. Students 
who fail to pay the examination fee will not be permitted to write the 
examination.

Examination fees will not be refunded if a student who paid 
the fees leaves the college before the examination. The examination 
fees paid by students detained from writing examinations will not be 
refunded as per the guidelines of University of Madras. 
Reappearance for Arrears 
Students who failed in current papers can reappear in the 
subsequent examinations. A student can appear for a maximum of 
10 arrear papers in a semester. However, there is no restriction for 
the number of papers in arrears for the outgoing students in the last 
semester (sixth semester for UG and fourth semester for PG). 
Revaluation Regulations 
To apply for revaluation, students have to first apply for 
photocopy of their answer scripts within five days after the 
publication of the results. They need to get endorsement from any 
one of their teachers after showing them the answer scripts for 
possibility of revision of marks if they apply for revaluation. They 
have to apply for revaluation, if needed, within five days after 
receiving the photocopy of answer scripts. 
A student is eligible to apply for revaluation only for the 
current papers. If there is no change in the marks after the 
revaluations, the original marks will remain as final marks.

New College Students’ Union 
The New College Students' Union is an association that

the current students at the college are members of the Students'

there is a separate Association for Postgraduate Students. The

the election conducted at the beginning of every academic year.

nominated every year by the Principal from among the Secretaries

magazine). In the absence of suitable candidates among the 
Secretaries of Sectional Associations, the Principal is empowered to 
nominate representatives from the other students for the above 
positions.

National Service Scheme (NSS) 
The National Service Scheme (NSS) is a programme 
organized by the Ministry of Education and Youth Services affiliated 
to the Government of India to offer opportunities and training in 
service to the community and the nation for students with an aptitude 
for social service. It is intended to involve the college students in a 
variety of social service and developmental activities concomitantly 
with the normal academic programme. The motto of the scheme is 
“Education through Community Service.” Students of the first and 
second year Degree classes are eligible for admission. A total of 400 
students will be enrolled in the four Units of NSS working in the 
college under four programme officers. 
The NSS aims to develop in its volunteers the following: 
▪ A spirit of selfless service to our people in distress, poverty 
and ignorance, irrespective of region, language, religion and 
caste. 
▪ A capacity to identify and study community problems, to 
plan and to carry out welfare projects as a team. 
▪ A willingness to share the benefits of modern higher 
education with our people who did not get this benefit. 
▪ A dignity for manual work and respect and concern for our 
rural masses and 
▪ A living faith in the unity of India and our great national 
heritage. 
▪ Four units of NSS are function with 100 Volunteers per unit. 
Various developmental activities are conducted in the 
adopted villages annually, thereby creating permanent assets 
for the welfare of the needy.
